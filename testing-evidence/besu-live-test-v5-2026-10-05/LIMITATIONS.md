# LIMITATIONS — round 5 (contracts v2.1.0)

- **What was tested:** the uncommitted v2.1.0 working tree of `contracts/` (hashes in `evidence/00-baseline/r5-plan-contracts-sha256.txt`). A later change of those files invalidates the match. The Persian `contracts-fa/` were not compiled or compared.
- **Baseline vs test-fork evidence must not be merged.** Baseline (real constants, real calendar months): Net-R5-A and Net-R5-B. Test-fork (900 s months; five Registry time constants and probation/recovery overlays compressed): Net-R5-F — month crossings while the chain runs, the delivery-dispute vote, the appeal vote and the recovery hook. Nothing in Net-R5-F says anything about the real 7-day/72-hour durations.
- **No real calendar month boundary was crossed.** Net-R5-B has a back-dated genesis (previous month), Net-R5-F compresses the month. The real calendar algorithm `monthIdOf` was exercised only through its value at the genesis timestamp (24321 for October 2026, 24320 for September — checked against an independent JS calculation) and through `currentMonthId()` at live blocks; month lengths other than the current ones (28/31 days, year change) were not exercised.
- **`refreshBoard(address[])`**: the `votersToSync` list was exercised with validators and a non-validator in it (no effect expected and none seen); a case in which the Registry hook *failed* and a stale counter had to be repaired by the list could not be produced (the hook forwards 600,000 gas and nothing in the tests made it fail).
- **Ties:** one tie for the fifth seat was tested (older activation wins); ties at other positions and ties among more than two candidates were not.
- **Floor of 1 validator:** exercised with the ordinary exit/suspension paths and QBFT with one validator for about 18 seconds (≥ 6 blocks). Long-run behaviour of a one-validator network, the interaction with the RewardRouter, and a validator set shrinking while blocks are in flight were not tested. The exit of the board's members above the floor leaves the board with fewer than five seats (no outside candidate to fill); the "fewer than 3 seated members halts treasury payments and key rotations" rule was not exercised in this round.
- **Delivery-dispute and appeal votes:** one case (X) on the test-fork, one subject with stake (1% slash = Treasury transfer observed), snapshots tested for a validator activated after the filing (dispute), a validator recovered after the filing (appeal) and a validator suspended after the filing (dispute). The mass-failure exemption path, the expiry paths (`resolveDeliveryDisputeIfExpired`, `resolveAppealIfExpired`), `executeUncontestedSlash` with a non-zero stake and `recordPreExitViolation` were not exercised in this round (the uncontested slash ran once for a founder with zero stake to clear its case).
- **IdentityRegistry and ServiceStaking** (changed in v2.1.0: burned migrated identity, stake under withdrawal not counted) were **not tested** here beyond `registerIdentity` being used by the voters.
- **A14 (unexpected storage)**: only the live leg (block-0 stateRoot equals the MPT root of the genesis alloc) was run for the three new genesis files; the static scan against an independently derived expected alloc was **not** re-written for the v2.1.0 layout.
- **Group A** was run (58/58 on Net-R5-A, 58/58 on Net-R5-B, 59/59 on Net-R5-F) on pristine networks; Net-R5-A's and Net-R5-B's runs used the script before its fork extension (DEVIATIONS item 6).
- Everything is single-host, multi-process; the production genesis tool, RewardRouter, production gasLimit and the dashboard remain open project items. This is not a production-readiness statement.
