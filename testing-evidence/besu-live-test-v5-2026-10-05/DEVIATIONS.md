`besu-test-v5/` (tools copied from v4, junctions to the same Besu binary and node_modules). v2.1.0 changed the storage layout and the helpers, so the builder was rewritten (`r5-gen-seeds.js`, `r5-build-one.js`): the three **production** helpers are used as they are, only their FILL_IN placeholders are replaced (7 founder addresses, 5 board addresses = founders 1,2,4,6,7 as in the production helper, 15 DAO members, the genesis timestamp). ## 3. "Previous month" network (Net-R5-B) is a back-dated genesis, not a live crossing — [this session's own judgment call]
The month boundary on **baseline** contracts cannot be crossed in a few hours. Net-R5-B therefore has its genesis timestamp on 2026-09-30 23:59:00 UTC (the real clock is October), so the seeded `boardMonthId` (September) is older than the live month from block 1. This tests the contract's behaviour for a board whose month has passed, with real `block.timestamp`s — it is not a boundary crossed by a running chain. (Side effect: genesis→block-1 gap of about five days; time-based registry fields seeded from the genesis timestamp are old; none of the Net-R5-B checks depends on them.)
