## Discrepancy found (A09) — identical on every network built by this test builder

`ValidatorsBoard.lastBoardRefreshAt()` is **0** on every network, while the genesis-builder spec (§4.2.1, assertion list: `ValidatorsBoard.lastBoardRefreshAt() == network.genesisTimestamp`, P01, when the board is seeded) expects the genesis timestamp. The test builder (`testonly-build-one*.js`) seeds the board members and flags but never writes `lastBoardRefreshAt`. Consequence: the first `refreshBoard()` is allowed immediately instead of after 30 days. The value was already 0 on the original networks (Net-B, D, L01, L02, L04, L05, Fork: `A09.lastBoardRefreshAt = 0` in their `*-groupA.json`), where the spec comparison required by the brief's A09 row had not been made. Classification: test-tool gap against the spec (not a contract defect); no executed test depended on `refreshBoard()`.
