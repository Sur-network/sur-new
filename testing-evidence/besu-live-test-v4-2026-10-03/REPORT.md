## Compressed-constants table (test-fork only; baseline networks use the real values unmodified)

| Constant | Real value | Fork value | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MIN_DISTRIBUTION_INTERVAL` | 23 hours | 90 s | smallest tier, independently distinguishable from the two below |
| `RATE_CHANGE_DELAY` | 7 days | 180 s | "execution delay" tier |
| `MIN_RATE_CHANGE_LEAD_BLOCKS` | 201,600 blocks (~7 days @3s) | 80 blocks (~240 s @3s) | "height distance" tier — a block count, kept distinct in both value and unit from the delay above |

`PROPOSAL_EXPIRY`/`RATE_VOTING_EXPIRY` (30d), `SHARE_CHANGE_MIN_INTERVAL` (180d),
`CAP_CHANGE_TIMELOCK_DELAY` (7d), `BOARD_REFRESH_INTERVAL` (30d) were **left at their real
values** on the fork too — no listed test ID in this round's scope needs them compressed (§6.3
already marks their elapsed-time behavior as out of reach even under the document's own 14-day
path L, so no fidelity is lost by leaving them real).

**On the phrase "fork-verified equivalent" used below:** it means the contract *mechanism itself*
was exercised completely and produced a real, on-chain state change (e.g. D04's second
distribution actually happened; D05's backlog actually settled) — only the *wall-clock duration*
of the gate before it was compressed. It does **not** mean "equivalent to running it at the real
duration" in every respect, and it never applies to a step that was not actually exercised at all
(see C-L05-4's split rows below, where the L4 effectiveness step was never attempted — fork or
otherwise — and is marked NOT-RUN, not "fork-verified").
